\cvblock{Working Papers}{%
  \papertitle{Beyond the Label: Do Ecodesign Regulations Reduce Real-World Energy Use?} \textbf{[Job Market Paper]}

  \vspace{0.04in}
  I study the 2021 European washing-machine energy-label reform. Firms improve Eco efficiency while energy use rises in other wash programs. An equilibrium model of product design, purchases, and program choice quantifies the implications for actual electricity use and welfare.


  \smallvsp
  \papertitle{Does Carbon Pricing Misallocate Clean Technology? Evidence from Global \mbox{Shipping}}

  \vspace{0.04in}
  I study whether geographically fragmented carbon pricing reallocates cleaner ships across routes. I use global vessel movements and emissions data around the extension of the EU Emissions Trading System to maritime transport to examine how carriers adjust vessel deployment.
